# Loading screen ideas

Candidates to find and add to the library. Any length of source works: `pnpm encode` fits it to at most 15 seconds. Pick the best stretch with `--start` and `--end` rather than speeding up a long video a lot.

All of these are copyrighted footage. They're fine for a personal library, but bundling any of them in a public release needs permission from the rights holder. Fan recreations are still based on the original art. Screens that are simple to recreate from scratch (like the ZX Spectrum and C64 tape loaders) avoid the issue.
